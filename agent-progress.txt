Swift Deep Research v2 — Agent Progress Log
=============================================

PROJECT OVERVIEW
----------------
Complete rebuild of an existing macOS 14 / Swift 5.9 single-agent research app into a macOS 26 / Swift 6.2
orchestrator-worker deep research agent. Architecture mirrors Anthropic's multi-agent research system
(orchestrator + parallel worker subagents) with Foundation Models as the on-device orchestrator brain
and Anthropic/OpenAI/Gemini as long-context synthesizers.

CURRENT STATE OF REPO
---------------------
Old single-agent app (~6,235 LOC) still in place. Will be replaced incrementally; legacy files
deleted only after their replacements are wired and the build is green.

Existing dependencies (kept): SwiftSoup, mlx-swift, mlx-swift-examples, swift-markdown-ui,
ollama-swift.

To be added: swift-anthropic, MacPaw/OpenAI, mcp-swift-sdk, firebase-ios-sdk (for Firebase AI Logic
Gemini fallback).
